================================================================
         EXPORT CURRENCY TOOL — USER GUIDE
================================================================

WHAT IS THIS?
-------------
A tool for exporters to track foreign currency payments (USD/EUR/GBP).
When your bank converts your export payment to INR, this tool tells you
if the bank gave you a fair rate — or if you lost money.


================================================================
TOP BAR — LIVE RATES
================================================================

Shows live USD, EUR, GBP rates against INR (mid-market/true rate).
Refreshes every 5 minutes automatically.
Click [ Refresh rates ] to update manually.


================================================================
3 TABS
================================================================

  Invoices          → Enter & track all invoices
  Reports           → Monthly summaries & bank comparison
  Rate Trend & Alerts → Rate chart + set price alerts


================================================================
TAB 1 — INVOICES
================================================================

STEP 1 — FILL THE INVOICE FORM
--------------------------------
  Invoice No.      — Your reference number (optional)
  Customer         — Buyer name
  Currency         — USD / EUR / GBP
  Amount Received  — Foreign currency amount
  Received Date    — Date you lodged the bill with the bank
  Spot Rate        — Auto-filled from live rate (edit if needed)
  CC Interest %    — Your packing credit loan rate (default 9.5%)
  Bank Name        — Which bank (helps compare banks later)
  Bank Call Date   — Date bank actually converted the money
  Bank Rate        — Rate the bank gave you
                     Leave EMPTY if bank hasn't converted yet → invoice stays Pending


STEP 2 — READ THE LIVE CALCULATION
------------------------------------
As you type, the tool calculates:

  (A) Spot conversion    → Amount × Spot rate  (what you should have got)
  Days held (N)          → Days between received date and bank date
  CC interest cost       → Interest paid on your CC loan for those N days
  Break-even rate        → Minimum rate the bank must give you to avoid a loss
  (B) Bank conversion    → Amount × Bank rate
  Net gain / loss        → (B − A) minus CC cost  ← your real profit or loss

VERDICT:
  GREEN  → You made a profit
  RED    → You made a loss (bank rate was below break-even)
  YELLOW → Bank rate not entered yet — shows the break-even rate to ask the bank for

WHAT-IF TABLE:
  Shows the break-even rate on Day 0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 10 after receipt.
  Helps you decide: should I wait for a better rate or convert now?


STEP 3 — SAVE & MANAGE
------------------------
  [ Save Invoice ] — Saves to the list below
  [ Clear ]        — Reset the form
  [ Edit / Del ]   — Edit or delete from the saved table

FILTERS on the table: search by name, filter by status/currency/bank/date.
Click any column header to sort.

EXPORT:
  [ Export CSV ]    — Download as Excel file
  [ Backup JSON ]   — Full backup of all data
  [ Import Backup ] — Restore from backup


================================================================
TAB 2 — REPORTS
================================================================

Shows only completed invoices (bank rate entered).

Breakdown by: Month / Customer / Currency / Bank
Each shows total gain, loss, and net result.

BANK COMPARISON TABLE — shows which bank gave you the best rate
on average. Use this data when negotiating with your bank.


================================================================
TAB 3 — RATE TREND & ALERTS
================================================================

TREND CHART
  30-day line chart for USD/EUR/GBP.
  Shows if today's rate is above or below the 30-day average.
  Use this to decide the right time to ask the bank to convert.

RATE ALERTS
  Set a target rate. When the rate hits it, you get a notification.
  Example: "Alert me when USD/INR reaches 86.00"
  Checked every 5 minutes. Allow browser notifications for pop-ups.


================================================================
DATA
================================================================

All data is saved to the cloud (Firebase) in real time.
Opens on any device with the same data.
Also saved locally in the browser as a backup.


================================================================
FORMULAS (quick reference)
================================================================

  A  =  Amount × Spot rate
  B  =  Amount × Bank rate
  CC cost  =  A × (CC% ÷ 100) ÷ 365 × N days
  Break-even  =  Spot × (1 + CC% ÷ 100 × N ÷ 365)
  Net  =  (B − A) − CC cost


================================================================
TIPS
================================================================

  • Enter the invoice the same day you lodge it with the bank.
  • Keep Bank Rate blank until the bank converts — use break-even
    to negotiate.
  • Always enter the bank name — Reports will show which bank
    performs best after a few months.
  • Export CSV every month for your accounts.
